Tracks and Clips

方以类聚，物以群分。

Book of Changes, “Appended Judgments I” (《周易·系辞上》)

Translation: “Things gather according to their kind; creatures divide according to their group.” Tracks and clips find their place by kind; clear grouping is what makes editing orderly.

The arrangement area is Yeban's main workspace: tracks as rows, clips laid out on the timeline to their right. This chapter covers creating tracks, placing clips and turning a piece of material into the arrangement you want.

Track Types and the Track Header

Three track types

The Track menu offers three creation entries:

  • New MIDI Synth Track (Cmd/Ctrl+N): sounds via a built-in synthesizer or plugin instrument, holding MIDI clips.
  • New Audio Track: holds recorded or imported audio clips.
  • New Aux Bus Track: receives sends from other tracks for summing and group processing.

Delete Selected Track removes the selected track from the menu.

The track header

Each track's left side is the header: name, mute/solo switches, volume, and the Track Menu button at the very front (collecting that track's operations). Mute silences the track; solo listens only to it; both can also be operated in the mixer console (Chapter 7).

Create, Move, Copy and Split Clips

Create a clip

Once a clip is created on a track it appears on the timeline. Its content depends on the track type: notes on a MIDI track (written in the piano roll, Chapter 4), audio material on an audio track. An empty MIDI clip defaults to four bars, its tick length derived from the project's time signature; if the time signature is invalid the app reports “invalid time signature, cannot compute clip length” instead of inventing a length.

Move, resize and cut/copy/paste

  • Move: drag to reposition; drag either edge to resize.
  • Copy / Paste / Cut: Cmd/Ctrl+C, Cmd/Ctrl+V, Cmd/Ctrl+X, matching the Edit menu group.
  • Split: select a clip and press Cmd/Ctrl+T to cut it at the playhead.

Dragging previews the destination before you release; you can cancel mid-drag without leaving a half-finished state.

Common clip flow: create $$ place $$ copy $$ split $$ write notes

Stretch, Merge and Multi-select Editing

Dragging a clip's edge changes only its time span, not the pitch or relative timing of its notes (for audio, it changes the playback range). To join adjacent clips into one continuous piece, align their boundaries first, then merge; the two clips become one and undo restores them.

With several clips selected, copy, cut and delete apply to the whole group. If only part of a group is selected, the operation follows the selection and does not touch unselected clips.

Clip Parameters and the Clip Launch Matrix

Selecting a clip shows its identity (name, owning track) and editable parameters in the inspector/clip parameter area. Press Tab to open the Clip Launch Matrix (Session view): the interface becomes a grid of clips; click a cell to fire that clip, which suits live launching and improvised arrangements. F5 also switches to Session view; F6 returns to the Arrangement view.

Cross-track Drag and Track Change

In the current version, dragging a clip changes only its horizontal position within the same track. Moving a clip from one track to another is still in evolution (work item T-FIX-209). So do not treat “drag onto another track” as available: to place material on another track, copy/paste it onto the target track and delete the original until the capability officially lands. Yeban does not disguise an unimplemented operation as a success.
