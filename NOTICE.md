This software was developed as part of the Yeban DAW project, dedicated to creating a modern, high-performance, deterministic desktop digital audio workstation powered by Rust and Slint.

This product includes software developed by third-party free and open-source projects:
- Slint UI framework, developed by SixtyFPS GmbH (GPL-3.0 / Commercial)
- cpal cross-platform audio library, maintained by the RustAudio team (Apache-2.0)
- rtrb lock-free real-time ring buffer, developed by Stefan Floeren (MIT / Apache-2.0)
- symphonia media decoding library, developed by Philip Deljanov (MPL-2.0)
- midly zero-allocation MIDI parser, developed by Mehdi Ben Rayana (MIT / Apache-2.0)
- rubato sample rate converter, developed by Henrik Enquist (MIT)
- signalsmith-stretch, developed by Geraint Luff (MIT)
- Basic Pitch audio-to-MIDI system, developed by Spotify (Apache-2.0)
- DeepFilterNet speech enhancement, developed by Hendrik Schröter / FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg (MIT / Apache-2.0)
